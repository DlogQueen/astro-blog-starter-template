Hey y'all, and welcome! I'm Keisha, and I've been pouring my heart (and a whole lot of ribbon) into **The Glitzy Farmhouse** — handmade designer wreaths and seasonal decor, made right here in Dothan, Alabama.

Every piece is built by hand, one at a time. No two wreaths ever come out exactly alike, and that's the fun of it — you tell me the colors, the season, the vibe, and I'll bring it to life for your front door.

This little corner of the internet is where I'll be posting:

- **Upcoming market dates** and local pop-ups
- **Charity raffles and giveaways** I'm donating pieces to
- **Restocks** on ribbon, mesh, and florals when new shipments come in
- **Custom order openings** for each season

Thank you for supporting a small, local, handmade business. It means the world. Come say hi on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/the_glitzy_farmhouse/) any time — I love seeing what colors and styles y'all are dreaming up next.

— Keisha
