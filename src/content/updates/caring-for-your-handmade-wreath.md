Your wreath is handmade to last, but a little care goes a long way. Here's how to keep it looking fresh:

## Outdoor wreaths

- Hang it somewhere with at least partial cover — direct sun and rain will fade ribbon and florals faster.
- After storms or high wind, give it a quick shake-out and re-fluff the bow.
- Bring it inside during severe weather if you can.

## Storage between seasons

- Store in a large bag or bin somewhere cool and dry — a closet or spare room works better than a hot attic or garage.
- Avoid stacking heavy items on top, which can flatten the shape.
- A gentle re-fluff of the mesh and florals when you bring it back out will make it look brand new.

## General upkeep

- Spot clean with a soft, dry cloth — avoid soaking ribbon or mesh.
